TYPE: BIKE
DURATION: 90 minutes

NOTES:
POINTS RACE - 12 scoring opportunities (6 climbs, 6 sprints)
Course: Rising Empire, 42km, 755m elevation, ~60-75 min
Power-ups: Feather (climbs), Draft (recovery/positioning), Anvil (situational)

PRE-RACE PREP (60-90 min before):
- Light meal 2-3 hours before (familiar foods, easy to digest)
- Hydration: 16-20oz water + electrolytes starting 2 hours before
- Carb drink: Start sipping 60 min before race

WARMUP (20-25 min):
- 10 min easy spin 150-180W
- 5 min progressive 180-240W
- 3x 15-sec sprints @ 450W+ (3 min recovery between)
- 2x 1-min @ 320W (neuromuscular primer)
- 5 min easy spin back to start

RACE STRATEGY - LAP 1 (Learning):
- First 3-5 min: Survive chaos, find good wheels
- Scoring climbs: Test your legs, don't empty tank
- Pick 2-3 scoring opportunities to contest
- Learn who is strong, who is fading
- Use Feather power-up on climbs (your strength at 4.3 w/kg)

RACE STRATEGY - LAP 2 (Executing):
- Target 3-4 climbs for points (your wheelhouse)
- Be selective on sprints - only if positioned well
- Expect surges to 350-400W for 30-60 sec on climbs
- Threshold efforts 280-300W between scoring
- Save one big match for final scoring opportunity

POWER PACING:
- Opening 5 min: Expect 300-350W chaos
- Cruise between scoring: 260-280W
- Climb attacks: 320-380W for 30-90 sec
- Sprint contests: 450W+ for 15-30 sec
- Expected NP: 280-300W for full effort

NUTRITION DURING:
- Carb drink throughout (aim 40-50g carbs)
- Sip between scoring efforts
- Electrolytes critical (remember cramping issues)

POST-RACE:
- 10-15 min easy cooldown spin immediately
- Recovery nutrition within 30 min (carbs + protein)
- Hydration + electrolytes
- Hot shower to drop core temp by 9:30pm
- Screen time cutoff 10pm (no race analysis/scrolling)
- Lights out by 10:30pm for 8+ hours sleep

TACTICAL NOTES:
- Don't try to win all 12 scoring opportunities
- Points accumulate - consistency beats big risks
- Your climbing power (4.3 w/kg) is your weapon
- Let pure sprinters take flat sprints
- Focus on climb points where you're strongest
